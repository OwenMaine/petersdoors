Who We Are

PETER'S DOORS, INC. is a Miami-based, family-run company dedicated to delivering top-quality doors and expert installations for both homes and businesses. With decades of experience, our team is passionate about pairing security with style, helping you create welcoming entrances and robust, dependable spaces.

What We Do

  • Residential Doors: Entry, patio, and interior doors designed for elegance and security.
  • Commercial Solutions: Sturdy, code-compliant doors for offices, storefronts, warehouses, and more.
  • Custom Design & Installation: Tailored door solutions that fit any space perfectly.
  • Repairs & Upgrades: Fast, reliable service to keep your doors functional and stylish.
